How to apply

Samsung Galaxy (One UI)
  1. Install Good Lock and Theme Park from the Galaxy Store.
  2. Open Theme Park > Icon > Create new > Icon pack and pick Labellum.
  3. Tap save, name your set, then tap Apply.
Nova, Lawnchair, Niagara, Smart Launcher
  1. Open the pack app, tap Apply and pick your launcher.

Missing an icon?

In the app, open the menu and tap Icon Request, tick the apps you want and send. We draw them for the next update. Questions: request@labellum.eu
